The gap

Even Etsy admits its calculator doesn't tell you this.

Etsy's built-in profit estimate is a starting point, not the real number. In its own Seller Handbook, Etsy explains that the estimate leaves out exactly the fee that hurts the most.

Etsy's Seller Handbook states plainly that its profit calculator only offers a basic estimate — and does not account for fees that vary order to order, including Offsite Ads, which can add up to 15% on top of everything else.

Automatic

Connect via Etsy's official login

We'll use Etsy's own developer platform (OAuth) — the same secure login Etsy provides to trusted apps. Your shop syncs on its own, no exporting required. We never see or ask for your Etsy password.

Always available

Or upload your CSV, no connection needed

Prefer not to connect anything? Export your order history from Etsy and upload it directly. Nothing to authorize, nothing to revoke later.

One honest catch: Etsy's own systems don't cleanly link every Offsite Ads fee to the order it came from — even large seller tools run into this. Your CSV export happens to label these more precisely than the automatic connection can right now, so if Offsite Ads accuracy matters most to you, CSV upload will be the more exact option at launch. We'll keep improving the automatic sync as Etsy's platform allows.

What's next

One shop today. Sharper tools as we go.

We're not going to chase every marketplace at once. Here's the order we're tackling real, known gaps — starting with the one under our own feet.

Now

Connect your Etsy shop

OAuth sync via Etsy's own official login, or upload a CSV — no password sharing either way. This is the foundation everything else builds on, so it comes first, not last.

🔧 Shipping with early access
Next

Add your own costs

Etsy fees are only part of the story — materials, packaging, and shipping matter too. We're adding a simple cost-per-listing field so your number can reflect what you actually spent, not just what Etsy took. Optional, never required.

🎯 The honest gap: without this, we show profit after fees — not full profit
Next

Closing the Offsite Ads gap

Etsy's own systems don't cleanly link every ad fee to the order it came from — even large seller tools only get partial matches. We're building smarter timing-and-amount matching, plus a one-tap "associate manually" option for anything that doesn't auto-link.

📊 Found in our own research — Etsy Support confirms this isn't fully solved platform-wide
Next

Refunds, tracked properly

Etsy doesn't tell any third-party tool which refund belongs to which order. We're building a simple manual-match flow so a refunded sale doesn't quietly throw off a listing's margin.

📊 Same root cause as the Ads gap — Etsy's ledger doesn't expose this link yet
Later

Send it straight to your books

Export profit data directly to QuickBooks, or a clean accounting-ready CSV. Same shop, deeper tools — not a reason to add another platform to manage.
